# Code of Conduct

This applies to every chunkdb repository, issue, pull request, and discussion.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Accept feedback on your own work.
- Keep discussion on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules.
They apply the rules fairly and keep reports private.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers at nitikaavoloshin@gmail.com.
